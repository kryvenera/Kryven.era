Cookie Policy

Last updated:

This page explains what is stored on your device and which third parties may be contacted when you use KRYVEN ERA.

1. Essential storage (always on)

These are needed for the store to work and do not require consent: your bag and wishlist, saved checkout details (draft), sign-in session and Customer ID, discount code in use, pending payment reference, and your cookie choice. They live in your browser's local/session storage and are not used for advertising.

2. Optional third-party content (only with your consent)

3D product viewer: loads the three.js library from cdn.jsdelivr.net.
Delivery map: loads map code from unpkg.com and map tiles / address look-up from OpenStreetMap. If you press "Use my location", your browser asks permission first.
These providers can see your IP address and browser details. If you reject, the rest of the store works normally and the 3D view and map stay off.

3. Third-party services needed to run the store

Supabase (database and sign-in), Cashfree (payment page and verification), Google Fonts (typefaces), Unsplash (product images). They may receive your IP address when pages load. Payment and sign-in features cannot work without their respective providers.

4. Analytics and advertising

We currently do not use analytics, advertising or social-media tracking cookies. If we add them, they will stay off until you accept them.

5. Your choices

Use the "Cookie settings" link in the footer to change your choice at any time. You can also clear storage in your browser settings; this will empty your bag and sign you out.
