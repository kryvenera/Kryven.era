Refund & Return Policy

Last updated:

We want you to be happy with your order. This policy explains cancellations, returns, exchanges and refunds.

1. Cancellations

You can cancel an order before it is shipped by contacting us with your order ID. Once shipped, please use the return process below.

2. Returns & exchanges

Request a return or exchange within days of delivery from the Help & Care page or WhatsApp, with your order ID. Items must be unused, unwashed, with tags and in original packaging. Items that arrive damaged, defective or wrong must be reported with photos within the same period; we will arrange pickup or replacement at no cost to you.

3. Non-returnable items

Items marked as final sale on the product page, and items that show signs of wear, washing or alteration.

4. Refunds

After the returned item passes a quality check, the refund is sent to your original payment method within business days. For Cash on Delivery orders we will ask for UPI or bank details to send your refund. If you paid a COD advance, it is refunded in the same way when the order is cancelled by us or returned as above.

5. Shipping charges

Shipping paid at checkout is refunded only if the return is due to our error (wrong, damaged or defective item).
