2. What we collect and why

Account: name, mobile number and optional email, to create and secure your account (legal basis: your consent).
Orders & delivery: name, mobile number, delivery address, landmark (optional), city, state, pincode and the items you order, to process, ship, track and support your order.
Payments: processed by our payment provider (Cashfree). We do not see or store your card number, UPI PIN or OTP. We keep the order ID, amount, payment status and method.
Bag, wishlist and preferences: stored on your device (and with your account where you are signed in) so the store works as expected.
Reviews: name, rating, text and any photos you choose to upload.
Optional location: if you use "Use my location" or the map pin at checkout, the coordinates you select are used only to fill in your delivery address.
Support: messages you send us on WhatsApp or email.

We collect only the data needed for these purposes. Email is optional. We do not collect data for advertising and we do not sell personal data.

3. Who we share it with

Only service providers needed to run the store, under their own privacy terms: Supabase (database and sign-in), Cashfree (payments), delivery/courier partners (name, phone, address), and hosting provider Vercel. If you accept optional third-party content, your browser also contacts cdn.jsdelivr.net (3D viewer) and OpenStreetMap / unpkg (delivery map). Product images and fonts may be loaded from Unsplash and Google Fonts, which can receive your IP address. We may disclose data if required by law.

4. Cookies and similar storage

See our Cookie Policy.

5. How long we keep data

Account data is kept while your account is active. Order, invoice and payment records are kept for as long as tax, accounting and consumer-protection law require. When data is no longer needed for its purpose it is deleted or anonymised.

6. Your rights

You can: get a summary of the personal data we hold; ask us to correct or update it; ask us to erase it; withdraw consent at any time (this does not affect past processing); nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become incapacitated; and make a grievance. You can edit details and delete your account from the Account page, or write to us using the contact details above. We aim to acknowledge requests within 48 hours and resolve them within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

7. Children

Our store is meant for adults. If you are under 18, please use it only with a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect a child's data without verifiable parental consent, and we do not track or target children.

8. Security

We use HTTPS, access controls and server-side payment verification. No online service is perfectly secure; if a data breach affects you we will notify you and the authorities as required by law. Never share OTPs, passwords, UPI PINs or card PINs with anyone.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version; material changes will be highlighted on the site.
