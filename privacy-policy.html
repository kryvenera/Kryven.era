Privacy & Customer Policy

Welcome to KRYVEN ERA. Your trust matters to us.

1. Your Account

We may collect your name, mobile number, email address when provided, and necessary account information to create and manage your account.

2. Verification & Security

Mobile number or email verification may be used for account security and important account communication. Never share OTPs, passwords, UPI PINs, ATM PINs, or card PINs.

3. Orders & Delivery

Delivery details are used to process, fulfil, track, and support your orders.

4. Payments

Payments may be processed by secure payment providers. KRYVEN ERA does not ask customers to share their UPI PIN, ATM PIN, or card PIN.

5. Wishlist & Shopping Activity

Wishlist, cart, and relevant shopping information may be stored to provide requested storefront features.

6. Account Controls

You can edit available account details, view your Customer ID, log out, and request account deletion from the available account controls.

7. Account Deletion & Records

When you delete an account, applicable account data may be removed. Certain records may need to be retained for completed orders, legal, accounting, security, or fraud-prevention requirements.

8. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated when necessary. Updated wording will be displayed on the website.

By continuing to use KRYVEN ERA account features, you confirm that you have read and understood this policy.
